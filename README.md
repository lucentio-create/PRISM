🎯 Project Mission

Privilege review is the most difficult art to master in document review. Reviewers either underflag or overflag, and either error can be catastrophic in terms of waiver of privilege — waiver isn't just embarrassing, it's often irreversible once documents are produced, and it drives up litigation costs and delays court proceedings. PRISM is a multi-agent system that I designed that runs first-pass analysis and flagging on privilege calls. But handling volume isn't the same as handling the decision — the most important part of privilege coding is the human in the loop.
